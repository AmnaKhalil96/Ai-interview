# Project brief

Job seekers preparing for interviews generally have two options: rehearse alone with no feedback, or find a friend, mentor, or paid coach willing to run a mock interview — a resource that's often unavailable, inconvenient to schedule, or simply out of reach, especially for people earlier in their careers or without a strong professional network. InterviewIQ closes that gap by using AI to generate interview questions tailored to the specific job a candidate is applying for, then giving structured, actionable feedback on each answer they give — the same kind of coaching a human mock-interviewer would provide, but available on demand, as many times as needed, at no cost beyond the candidate's own time. I chose this idea because the core value is real and personal: practice is what actually builds interview confidence and skill, and the biggest barrier to practicing isn't motivation, it's access to someone qualified to listen and give useful feedback — a problem AI is specifically well-suited to solve.
